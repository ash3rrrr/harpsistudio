## Arrangement

- Bars 1–4: chopped vocal hook, dark pad and a sweep into the first drop.
- Bars 5–8: half-time drop with vocal growl, sub and synth growl bass.
- Bars 9–12: drums and bass out; vocal hook and piano answer carry the break.
- Bars 13–16: second drop adds the vocal harmony and a final sweep.

The `.harp` project embeds the take, so it opens without the source WAV.
